Features

ChatGPT supports a wide variety of everyday study, writing, and productivity workflows, including:

  • Drafting and editing essays, emails, and study outlines
  • Explaining programming concepts and debugging syntax errors
  • Translating text and summarizing long articles into key takeaways

Because it adapts to the specific instructions and constraints in your prompt, you can tailor responses for beginners or advanced audiences.
