How It Works

ChatGPT is powered by a transformer-based neural network trained on a broad range of text to recognize language patterns, context, and intent.

When you enter a prompt, the model processes your input and predicts the most relevant sequence of tokens one step at a time until a complete, coherent response is formed.

To try ChatGPT yourself, visit https://chat.openai.com/, sign in to an account, and type a question or task into the prompt bar.
